1 · Background beside Style, Palette and Type — the Wave selected

The rail gains a fourth chip axis, Background, after Type: the seven backgrounds and None, each chip leading with a still swatch of its background the way a palette chip leads with its dot. The chosen background draws live behind the page’s own hero, the heading block; the Wave gathers its lines around it. The door is the header’s Design entry, drawn in its current-page treatment. Dashed borders mark what is new; they are not part of the design.
